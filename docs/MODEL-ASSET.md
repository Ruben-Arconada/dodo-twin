# Dodo Twin procedural asset

`lib/dodo/model.ts` is an original Three.js asset created for Dodo Twin. It contains no downloaded models, photographs, textures, fonts, or other third-party creative assets. All geometry, painted canvas textures, and anatomical shaping are generated by the source code. Three.js is the runtime dependency.

The model is an artistic anatomical reconstruction of the extinct **Raphus cucullatus**, not a claim of exact scientific soft-tissue reconstruction. Its silhouette uses a pear-shaped trunk, a rising tapering neck, small head, disproportionately large hooked bill with a dark base and light green–pale yellow distal horn, a bare grey face, small folded wings with lighter primaries, stout yellowish legs with black claws, three forward toes and one hallux per foot, and a tuft of curly light tail feathers (revision 02, 28 Sep 2026; evidence and artistic choices are separated in `docs/01-anatomia-y-evidencia.md`). The exhibit's 0.70 m crown height is a deliberately selected display scale.

## Coordinate contract

- Units: metres; up: +Y; forward: +Z.
- The lowest claw tips approach Y=0; the foot pads bear weight above that plane.
- Neutral height is approximately 0.70 m, including short crown feathers.
- Origin is centred between planted feet, on the floor.
- The mesh does not impose animation, render a scene, fetch resources, or start an event loop.

## Articulation contract

`createDodo()` returns `{ root, joints, skeleton, updateVisuals, dispose }`.

| Joint key | Axis | Attachment / intended motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `body_pitch` | X | Trunk relative to planted legs; pivot at pelvis |
| `neck_yaw` | Y | Neck base turning |
| `neck_pitch` | X | Neck base nodding |
| `head_yaw` | Y | Head turning at top of neck |
| `head_pitch` | X | Head nodding at top of neck |
| `beak` | X | Lower mandible; positive rotation opens downward |
| `wing_left` | Z | Left vestigial wing from the bird's own viewpoint |
| `wing_right` | Z | Right vestigial wing from the bird's own viewpoint |

Left is the bird's negative X side. The application uses mirrored left/right commands and a centralized axisSign = -1 for both wing joints so positive left-wing commands open the left wing outwards, while negative right-wing commands open the right wing outwards. All joint rest rotations are zero. Rest position, Euler rotation, quaternion, and named axis are also recorded in `joint.userData.rest` and `root.userData.dodo.restTransforms`. Add requested angles to the rest rotation; avoid accumulating an angle each frame.

The hidden `skeleton` overlay belongs to the root. Set `skeleton.visible = true` to display it; call `updateVisuals` after applying joint angles so its markers follow the current articulated pose. The skeleton renders through the body, with warm ivory pivot markers and amber segments.

`updateVisuals({ blink, breath, gazeX, gazeY })` accepts blink and breath in [0,1] and optional gaze in [-1,1]. A breath value of 0.5 is neutral. It applies subtle thorax scaling, eyelid closure, and eye offset without replacing any joint angles. Call it once per frame, after articulation.

`dispose()` releases the geometries, materials, and generated textures and removes the root from its parent. Canvas 2D is required when constructing the asset; construct it in browser lifecycle code rather than server-side rendering.

## Geometry and rendering

The torso, neck, skull, bill, thighs, legs and toes are continuous lofted or swept surfaces. Revision 02 counts: about 1,850 contour feathers on the trunk, 630 on the neck, 470 on crown and nape, and 300 per feathered thigh, all GPU-instanced. Each folded wing is built from about 40 shingled instanced coverts and a fan of 7 lighter primaries; there is no separate shoulder disc. The tail tuft has 22 curling plumes, each a swept rachis carrying instanced soft barbs (≈600 barbs in total). The bill is lofted from explicit side-profile rows (culmen, cutting edge, half-width) with vertex-coloured keratin zones, a long slit nostril and a gape line on each side. Fine feather barbs, horn grain and tarsal scales are painted into deterministic canvas textures. The outer feather edges come from geometry, avoiding alpha sorting and transparent feather cards.

Measured neutral envelope (actual vertices, not bounding boxes): 394 mm wide with folded wings, 696 mm high, 527 mm deep from the bill hook to the tail.

Use a physically based renderer with soft area or directional illumination, ambient fill, shadows, and a modest environment contribution. The source uses subdued grey-brown plumage with a darker dun wing, a dark proximal bill sheath fading to light green and pale yellow, and ivory-grey tail plumes. A camera around (1.0, 0.62, 1.2), looking at (0, 0.36, 0), gives an initial three-quarter exhibit view.

Large rotations can expose intersections because this is a rigid-part articulated exhibit asset, not a deforming skinned character. The only authoritative movement envelope is `lib/dodo/config.ts`; do not define a separate range in the asset. Current default ranges in degrees are: body pitch −7 to 7, neck yaw −35 to 35, neck pitch −18 to 22, head yaw −30 to 30, head pitch −25 to 28, beak 0 to 24, left wing −5 to 25, and right wing −25 to 5. These are provisional simulation limits, not mechanically verified ranges.
